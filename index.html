01 / ABOUT JEWELS

Our story. Our vision.

Discover the story behind Jewels Junior School, a family-centred Christian school committed to building tomorrow.

01 WHO WE ARE

Building a school with a vision.

Jewels Junior School was established in 2018. The idea originated from Nyasha and Tendai Vitorini as a family centred Christian School.

It was a Vision given to Tendayi who was a teacher by profession. The Vitorinis led by Nyasha then went to work in 2017 to build and establish the school.

The school is a family run institution through the Vitorini family trust.

2018

Established

Jewels Junior School was established in 2018.

Our journey so far.

The school was registered with the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education as an ECD centre in 2018, then an infant school in 2019.

From 2020 it became a Junior school enrolling learners from ECD to Grade 7.

Our examination board is ZIMSEC and our first class of Grade 7 candidates was registered in 2025 scoring a 100% pass rate.

03 / SCHOOL LIFE

Life at Jewels. Beyond the classroom.

A school day is more than lessons. It is the sound of children discovering something new, the excitement of an adventure, the joy of friendship, the pride of achievement and the memories created along the way.

EVERY DAY · A NEW MEMORY
01 THE JEWELS EXPERIENCE

A childhood worth remembering.

At Jewels Junior School, we want children to experience education as something they can live, feel and remember. From discovering Zimbabwean culture to travelling together, playing outdoors, celebrating achievements and simply having fun, every experience becomes part of their journey.

02 CULTURE & HERITAGE
ROOTED IN OUR HERITAGE

Knowing where we come from.

Our learners are given opportunities to experience and appreciate the culture, traditions and heritage that surround them.

LEARNING THROUGH EXPERIENCE

Tradition becomes a lesson.

Culture is not only something children read about. It becomes something they can see, touch, experience and understand.

03 ADVENTURE & DISCOVERY

The journey is part of learning.

There is something special about leaving the school grounds together. The anticipation, the conversations, the excitement of boarding a bus and discovering somewhere new all become part of the learning experience.

When the whole school comes together.
